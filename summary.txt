VerifierShift studies what task verification can establish when current policy evidence is missing or newly obtained. Three authored SQLite schemas yield24 paired observations and48 worlds with opposite outcomes within each incomplete-evidence pair. An authoritative read lets typed checks resolve48/48 complete cases, while168 actual model calls expose remaining interpretation errors: Gemma34/48 correct determinate decisions with7 false accepts, and Qwen15/48 with14 false accepts. The paper treats source validity, information acquisition, model errors and abstention separately. Its proposed follow-up builds versioned public-simulator evidence contracts and tests whether model reasoning adds independently verified coverage beyond strong typed checks.
