VerifierShift Restricted Evidence for Changed Agent Workflows
Saurav K. Aryal, Howard University. Updated November 1, 2026.

A frozen CPU-only replay of pinned public tau2 retail tools compares restricted predicate capsules with always-fresh, dependency/validity, sufficient-statistic and semantic controls. Forty provider-test tasks yield 31 eligible first order writes, 279 repeated task/edit pairs and 1,395 method decisions. Every method handles 217 supported decisions correctly and reviews 62 unsupported cases. Strong dependency, Z3 and compiled controls exactly match at 93 reads; sufficient statistics need 62, versus 217 always fresh. Mean serialized retention is 505 bytes capsule, 414 projection and 1,394 raw, including identical provenance. Projection wins bytes and reads. Four predicate bits without raw source scalars do not imply formal privacy. The value is an auditable interface and disclosure/coverage/refresh-cost tradeoff. This is not agent performance, full policy verification, natural drift or source-disjoint transfer.
